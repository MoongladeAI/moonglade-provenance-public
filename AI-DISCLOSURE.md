# AI contribution disclosure

Anthropic, OpenAI and Google model families helped with implementation, documentation, attack analysis and tests. Their outputs can be wrong. The human maintainer defines the project's goals and decides whether to release it.

All contributing model families are recused from release voting. Their confirmations are reviews, not votes. An independent agent audit can find defects, but final root approval belongs to the maintainer. Test results apply to the candidate and environments actually tested. This release makes no claim of improved AI performance or independent external evaluation.
